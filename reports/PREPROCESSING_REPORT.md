## 4. Target & Decision Leakage Prevention
To guarantee zero data leakage into model training, non-predictive keys and post-decision metadata columns were strictly removed from feature matrix $X$:
1. **`Sample_ID` (Database Key):** Excluded to prevent models from learning trivial sequence correlations or memorizing index positions.
2. **`Routing_Class` & `Routing_Class_Name`:** Target variables isolated strictly into label vector $y$.
3. **`Water_Quality_Class` (Legacy Label):** Excluded to eliminate redundant legacy bias.
4. **`Primary_Routing_Reason`, `Triggered_Parameters`, `Treatment_Required`, `Safety_Flag`:** Post-decision diagnostic metadata generated by the Phase 3 decision engine. Inclusion in $X$ would constitute direct target leakage.

*Archived audit: `reports/feature_leakage_audit.csv`*

## 5. Categorical Feature Handling
- **Variable:** `Greywater_Source` (4 discrete nominal categories).
- **Encoding Method:** **One-Hot Encoding**.
- **Rationale:** Label encoding assigns artificial numerical order ($0 < 1 < 2 < 3$) to nominal effluent origins that have no inherent hierarchy. One-Hot Encoding produces 4 distinct orthogonal binary indicators:
  - `Greywater_Source_Bathroom`
  - `Greywater_Source_Kitchen`
  - `Greywater_Source_Laundry`
  - `Greywater_Source_Mixed`
- **Leakage Safeguard:** Preprocessor category dictionaries are learned **strictly on the Training Set** and applied immutably across validation and test splits.

---

## 6. Numerical Feature Handling & Scaling Strategy
- **Scaling Decision:** **Passthrough (No Scaling Applied)**.
- **Scientific Justification:**
  - The primary classification algorithms specified for Phase 5 are **XGBoost** and **Random Forest**.
  - Decision tree architectures evaluate univariate split points along individual feature axes ($x_j \le \theta$). They are mathematically invariant to monotonic transformations, linear scaling (StandardScaler), or min-max normalization.
  - Preserving raw physical units ($\text{mg/L}$, $\text{NTU}$, $\text{CFU/100mL}$, $\text{pH}$) ensures direct interpretability of tree split thresholds and preserves natural physical meaning in SHAP waterfall plots and partial dependence plots.
- **Multicollinearity Review:** `DS_mg_L` and `TDS_mg_L` ($r = 0.994$) and `TUR_NTU` and `TSS_mg_L` ($r = 0.877$) are retained in the baseline feature matrix because tree ensembles perform well under collinearity without variance explosion. Redundancy will be analyzed during Phase 6 feature attribution.

## 7. Missing-Value Handling Strategy
- **Audit Result:** Audited missing counts across all 15 numerical parameters and categorical source: **0 missing cells (0.00% missingness)**.
- **Policy Statement:** `"No imputation required for the current dataset."`
- The `GreywaterPreprocessor` pipeline incorporates fallback safeguards (handling unseen categories via `handle_unknown='ignore'`) for future production telemetry without modifying historical data.

---

## 8. Outlier Policy
- **Audit Result:** Phase 2 identified statistical outliers via the IQR rule (notably 8.93% in `E_coli_CFU_100mL` and ~4% in `DS`/`TDS`).
- **Policy Statement:** `"Outliers are retained unless a value is demonstrated to be erroneous."`
- **Domain Justification:** In wastewater engineering, extreme contaminant spikes represent authentic operational hazards (e.g. concentrated grease discharge, surfactant peaks, or bacterial surges). Deleting or clipping these valid extreme values would prevent supervised models from learning the exact physical boundaries that trigger emergency `Sewer Bypass`.

## 15. Limitations & Recommendations for Phase 5
1. **Severe Minority Class (Indoor Reuse):**
   - Class 3 contains 4 samples in training, 1 in validation, and 1 in test.
   - Standard unweighted training may lead models to predict only Classes 0, 1, and 2 with zero recall on Class 3.
   - **Recommendation for Phase 5:** Evaluate class-weighted objective loss or focal loss, and monitor per-class macro-F1 and balanced accuracy rather than raw overall accuracy.
2. **Model Training Status:**
   - **Zero machine learning models were initialized or trained in this phase.**
   - All assets are positioned in strict alignment with Phase 5 requirements.
